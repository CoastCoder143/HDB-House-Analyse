## Key Observations from Examples

### CBD/Commercial Areas (Marina Bay, Orchard)
- ✅ Excellent MRT connectivity (multiple stations < 1km)
- ✅ High density of amenities (150-200+ found)
- ✅ Many shopping, dining, and entertainment options
- ✅ Limited residential schools
- ⚠️ Fewer parks and green spaces

### Residential Areas (Tampines, Punggol)
- ✅ Strong educational infrastructure (many schools)
- ✅ Good balance of amenities (80-200+ found)
- ✅ More parks and recreational facilities
- ✅ Community clubs and eldercare
- ✅ Family-oriented facilities (childcare, kindergartens)
- ⚠️ May require 1-2km travel to MRT

### Distance Patterns
- **Excellent**: < 500m to MRT, < 200m to bus
- **Good**: 500m - 1km to MRT, < 500m to bus
- **Acceptable**: 1-2km to MRT, < 800m to bus
- **Challenging**: > 2km to MRT

---

## Using This Information

When analyzing a house location, consider:

1. **Commute**: MRT distance for daily work travel
2. **Family**: Schools, childcare for children
3. **Daily Needs**: Supermarkets, hawker centres
4. **Healthcare**: Pharmacies, clinics (add to script)
5. **Recreation**: Parks, gyms, libraries
6. **Community**: Community clubs, eldercare
7. **Accessibility**: Bus stops, expressways

Compare multiple locations using these metrics to make informed decisions!
