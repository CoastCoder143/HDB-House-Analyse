## 📊 The Livability Score System

### Overall Score: 0-100 Points

The system evaluates 6 critical factors:

#### 1. Transport (25 points max) 🚇
**Why It Matters**: In Singapore, transport accessibility = property value

- **MRT within 500m**: 15 points (PRIME!)
- **MRT within 1km**: 12 points (Excellent)
- **MRT within 2km**: 8 points (Good)
- **Bus stops within 300m**: 7 points
- **Good distance from expressway**: 3 points (quiet but accessible)

**Agent Insight**: Properties within 500m of MRT command 10-15% premium!

#### 2. Education (20 points max) 🎓
**Why It Matters**: Families prioritize school access

- **Primary school within 1km**: 6 points
- **Childcare/Kindergarten within 500m**: 3 points
- **Library within 2km**: 3 points
- **Multiple schools**: Bonus points

**Agent Insight**: 1km to primary school = within balloting distance!

#### 3. Healthcare (15 points max) 🏥
**Why It Matters**: Essential for all demographics

- **Hospital/Clinic within 1km**: 5 points
- **Pharmacy within 500m**: 3 points
- **Eldercare facilities within 2km**: 2 points

**Agent Insight**: Aging population makes healthcare proximity crucial!

#### 4. Shopping & Daily Needs (20 points max) 🛒
**Why It Matters**: Daily convenience = quality of life

- **Hawker centre within 500m**: 8 points (Singapore essential!)
- **Supermarket within 500m**: 7 points
- **Shopping mall within 1km**: 5 points

**Agent Insight**: Hawker centres are uniquely Singaporean - highly valued!

#### 5. Recreation (10 points max) 🌳
**Why It Matters**: Lifestyle and wellness

- **Park within 500m**: 5 points
- **Community club within 2km**: 3 points
- **Gyms/Sports facilities within 2km**: 2 points

**Agent Insight**: Parks improve air quality AND property values!

#### 6. Safety (10 points max) 🛡️
**Why It Matters**: Deal-breakers exist

- **No dengue clusters**: 10 points
- **Dengue cluster within 500m**: -5 points (concern!)
- **No industrial areas nearby**: Bonus
- **Industrial area within 500m**: -3 points

**Agent Insight**: Dengue clusters can kill deals instantly!

### Why Hawker Centres Score High (8/20 points!)

In Singapore, hawker centres are:
- Cultural icons
- Affordable dining ($3-5 meals)
- Social hubs
- Daily necessity for many
- Highly valued by locals

**Agent Tip**: "Hawker centre nearby" resonates strongly with Singaporeans!

### Why MRT Matters Most (15/25 points!)

Singapore's public transport culture:
- Car ownership is expensive
- MRT is efficient and widespread
- "MRT within 500m" = prime location
- Property prices correlate with MRT proximity

**Agent Tip**: MRT proximity can justify 10-15% price premium!

### School Balloting Distance (1km rule)

For primary schools:
- Within 1km = Phase 2B priority
- Distance matters for balloting
- Families specifically search "within 1km"

**Agent Tip**: "1km to [School Name]" is a powerful selling point!
